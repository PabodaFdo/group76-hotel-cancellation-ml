02 / DATA UNDERSTANDING

Exploratory Data Analysis — Understanding Hotel Booking Patterns

Historical associations between booking characteristics and cancellations. Findings represent statistical patterns, not causal effects.

DISTRIBUTION

Booking Cancellation Distribution

The original dataset contains approximately 37% cancelled bookings.

Business insight: Cancellation risk is a significant factor for hotel occupancy forecasting and reservation planning.

LEAD TIME

Lead Time and Cancellation Risk

Bookings made further in advance have historically higher cancellation frequencies.

Business insight: Longer-lead-time reservations may benefit from additional monitoring or timely confirmation reminders.

HOTEL TYPE

Cancellation Rate by Hotel Type

City Hotels experience a notably higher cancellation rate compared to Resort Hotels.

Business insight: Different hotel types exhibit different historical cancellation patterns, so operational planning should consider property-specific booking behavior.

MARKET SEGMENT

Cancellation by Market Segment

Groups and Online TAs exhibit higher cancellation rates compared to Direct or Corporate bookings.

Business insight: Identifying segments with historically higher cancellation rates can help hotel managers prioritize reservation monitoring.

SEASONALITY

Seasonal Cancellation Patterns

Cancellation rates fluctuate by arrival month, demonstrating seasonal variation in historical demand.

Business insight: Seasonal booking patterns can inform staffing, room allocation and capacity planning.

05 / BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Business Insights & Operational Recommendations

Proposed applications of the prediction system for hotel management.

1. Proactive Reservation Follow-Up

Use elevated-risk predictions to prioritize optional booking confirmation reminders and appropriate customer communication.
Benefit: Helps staff focus attention on reservations requiring review.

2. Better Occupancy Planning

Use aggregate predicted cancellation-risk trends to support occupancy estimates and room inventory planning.
Benefit: Provides additional information for operational forecasting.

3. Smarter Staffing & Resources

Consider historical booking and cancellation patterns when planning housekeeping, front-desk staffing and resources.
Benefit: Supports more informed operational planning.

4. Market Segment Monitoring

Use historical EDA findings to identify booking segments that may require closer monitoring.
Benefit: Encourages segment-aware reservation management.

5. Human-Informed Support

Treat ML predictions as recommendations requiring appropriate human judgment.
Benefit: Reduces the risk of making consequential decisions solely from a model output.
